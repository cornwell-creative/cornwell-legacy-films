How It Works

Thoughtful from the first conversation through delivery.

Consultation

We talk through the people, place, history and purpose of the film.

Research

Photos, documents, records and pre-interviews help reveal the larger story.

Filming

Relaxed interviews are paired with cinematic images of meaningful places and details.

Archival Edit

Interviews, photographs, home movies and original footage are woven together.

Delivery

The finished documentary is prepared as a lasting part of the family or historical record.
