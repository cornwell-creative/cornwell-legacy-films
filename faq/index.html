Questions Before We Begin

What families usually want to know.

Legacy work is personal. These are the practical questions that usually come up before a project begins.

How long does a legacy film take?

The schedule depends on the number of interviews, research required, archival material and filming locations. A focused personal project can move faster than a multi-generation or historic-property documentary. The scope is established before production begins.

Do I need to prepare for the interview?

No script is required. We use discovery conversations and a loose story map so the interview feels natural while still covering the memories that matter.

What happens to old photographs and documents?

Archival materials are handled carefully and used only as needed for the film. Whenever possible, material can be digitized rather than remaining away from the family longer than necessary.

Can more than one family member be interviewed?

Absolutely. Many stories become richer when several people remember the same event differently. The number of interviews is part of the project scope.

Do you travel?

Yes. Cornwell Legacy Films is based in Natchez, Mississippi, and travel can be built into a project when the story requires filming elsewhere.

What if someone is uncomfortable on camera?

That is common. Interviews are conversational, unhurried and designed around the person rather than a performance. The goal is to capture a real voice, not create an on-camera personality.

Can the film include home movies, letters or recordings?

Yes. Home movies, audio recordings, letters, newspaper clippings, family records and photographs can all help build the historical and emotional context of a film.

What is the difference between Legacy Films and MyLifeToShare?

Legacy Films are broader documentary projects that may combine multiple interviews, places, research and archival material. MyLifeToShare is a more focused personal storytelling experience centered on one person's memories and reflections.

How is the finished film delivered?

Delivery is matched to the project and family. Digital files and private viewing options can be included, with archival or physical delivery discussed during planning.

Will my story be made public?

Not automatically. Privacy and public use are discussed before production. A family legacy project does not have to become public-facing content.

Still have a question?

The easiest way to start is to tell me what you are thinking about preserving. You do not need a finished plan.
